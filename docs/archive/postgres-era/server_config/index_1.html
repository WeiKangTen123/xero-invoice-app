02Why the gap opens up at 50 GB

At the 10 GB launch tier (see the main comparison doc) DigitalOcean is actually the cheaper of the two — $39/mo all-in vs Aliyun's ~$45/mo. The ranking flips once storage needs grow, because of a structural difference in how each provider sells database storage.

DigitalOcean — storage bundled with compute

You can't buy "50 GB" on its own. Storage is fixed per compute tier, and tiers roughly double at each step — so clearing 50 GB forces a jump to the tier with 60 GB, which also doubles the vCPU/RAM whether that compute is needed or not.

  • 1 vCPU · 1 GB RAM · 10 GB — $15/mo
  • 1 vCPU · 2 GB RAM · 30 GB — $30/mo
  • 2 vCPU · 4 GB RAM · 60 GB — $60/mo
  • 4 vCPU · 8 GB RAM · 115 GB — $120/mo

Aliyun — storage decoupled from compute

ApsaraDB RDS sells compute (vCPU/RAM) and cloud SSD storage as two separate line items. Need more disk but not more CPU? Add storage in ~20 GB increments at roughly $0.10–0.12/GB/mo without ever paying for compute you don't need.

  • 1 vCPU · 2 GB RAM (compute) — ~$25/mo
  • 2 vCPU · 4 GB RAM (compute) — ~$42/mo
  • + storage, any size — ~$0.10–0.12/GB/mo
  • + 50 GB example — ~$6/mo on top

At 100 GB, DigitalOcean is forced into its 4 vCPU/8 GB tier purely to get the disk space — compute it doesn't need. Aliyun just adds storage to the same 2 vCPU/4 GB instance. The gap compounds with every storage-driven tier jump on DO's side.

05Verdict

If a 50 GB+ database is the real target, size for Aliyun — but weigh the trade-off honestly.

  • 1Launching now, unsure of growth: stay on DigitalOcean's 10 GB tier ($39/mo). It's cheaper at small scale and the documentation/setup ease is worth more than the storage-pricing difference until data actually grows.
  • 2Already expecting 50 GB+ within the first year: Aliyun's decoupled storage saves real money and compounds further at 100 GB+ — worth the steeper setup curve, especially if any companies are Malaysia-based (KL data centre + residency).
  • 3Migration path: nothing locks you in — start on DigitalOcean, and if storage growth outpaces the 60 GB tier, this is the point to re-evaluate against Aliyun's per-GB model rather than paying for DO's next full compute jump.
⚠️ Prices above are estimates built from each provider's published tier structure, not live quotes — cloud pricing changes and varies by region/promo. Confirm current numbers on digitalocean.com/pricing and the Alibaba Cloud pricing calculator before committing budget.
